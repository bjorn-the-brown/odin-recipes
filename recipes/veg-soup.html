Vegetable Soup Recipe

About this Recipe

The humble veggie soup is truly the food of champions. This powerfully healthy meal is sure to sustain you, whatever battles you might face.

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp Butter
  • 2 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 2 Leeks, sliced
  • 3 large Carrots, diced
  • 2 cups peeled, chopped Butternut squash, in small pieces
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 1/2 tsp of your preferred seasoning mix
  • One can (410g) of diced Tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup Pearl Barley
  • 4 cups Vegetable Broth
  • 2 cups Water
  • 1 cup chopped Cabbage, Carrot Greens, Kale, or other greens

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat, then add the garlic and leeks and cook until soft and fragrant, 5 to 10 minutes.
  2. Add the carrots and squash, stirring to coat with butter, followed by the salt and seasoning.
  3. Add the tomatoes and barley, then pour in the broth and water.
  4. Let the soup simmer for about 45 minutes, then check to see if the barley is cooked through. If not, cook a little longer.
  5. Just before serving, add the chopped greens before serving.
